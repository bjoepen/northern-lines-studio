# Build 043 · Curated Welcome

Status: Implementation candidate

## Purpose

The Welcome page opens the narrative space of the Travelbook directly after the Cover. It is quieter than a destination page and must not become a second Cover.

## Binding rules

- Page type remains `welcome`, role remains `front_matter`.
- Existing Welcome grammar remains authoritative: Hero + Title + Introduction; Quote optional.
- Physical A5 paper remains white in Fjord and Ostsee.
- Typography is inherited exclusively from the active Editorial World.
- Hero is world-owned, illustrative and non-photorealistic in established Northern Lines language.
- No Companion on Welcome. First Companion appearance remains `journey_planning`.
- Existing Northern Lines footer and footer safe zone remain unchanged.
- No new layout variant, no DTP controls, no free positioning.
- Authored Introduction and Quote remain authoritative when present.

## Default editorial copy

Kicker: `Die Reise beginnt`

Title: `Willkommen`

Introduction fallback: `Orte, Licht und Erinnerungen – gesammelt für unterwegs.`

Quote fallback: `Jede Reise beginnt mit einer Ahnung von dem, was bleiben könnte.`
