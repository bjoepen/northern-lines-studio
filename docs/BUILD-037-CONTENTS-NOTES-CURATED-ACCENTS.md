# Build 037 — Inhaltsverzeichnis & Notizen · Curated Accents

Build 037 turns the previously generic Contents and Notes previews into two calm, production-oriented Northern Lines page grammars.

## Curated Accent

A **Curated Accent** is weaker than a Curated Hero. It is a fixed, world-owned editorial image used only to add atmosphere to utility pages without competing with their function.

Binding rules:

- world-owned, not project-owned;
- no picker, upload, replacement or visibility control;
- smaller and visually quieter than Curated Hero;
- yields space before navigation or writing capacity is reduced;
- literal white `#FFFFFF` physical page surface remains mandatory.

## Inhaltsverzeichnis

- automatically derived from the current travelbook page structure;
- typography and page navigation remain dominant;
- one small Curated Accent appears in the upper-right head area;
- Cover and the Contents page itself are omitted from the list;
- pages are grouped using the existing travelbook navigation sections;
- no user-authored contents payload is required.

## Notizen

- one large primary writing area;
- three calm secondary zones: **Schnellnotiz**, **Ideen**, **Skizze**;
- small Curated Accent in the head area;
- writing capacity always outranks the accent;
- Companion and Footer retain their protected zones;
- the page is a curated blank writing tool, not a form builder.

## Worlds

Fjord and Ostsee use the same grammar while receiving their own Curated Accent assets from the Design Library.

No `.nls` migration is required. The feature is system-driven and introduces no user-selectable assets.
