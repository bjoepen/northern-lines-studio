# ECR-022 – Destination Imagery Foundation / Image Composition Fix

**Status:** Approved / Implemented for Build 022 Final

## Request

Build 022 shall introduce real Destination imagery while preserving Northern Lines Studio's Product DNA and shall finalize the image composition after real Bergen imagery exposed the limits of rigid image boxes.

## Approved scope

- two semantic image roles: **Weite** and one shared portrait role for **Bild links / Bild rechts**;
- simple **Bild auswählen … / Bild ersetzen … / Bild entfernen** workflow;
- contextual `?` geometry help;
- project-local image copies inside `.nls`;
- fast Studio preview without crop;
- format `.nls` 0.9.0;
- neutral-white Fjord Destination surface;
- real images composed directly on the paper surface, without coloured image-card backgrounds;
- intrinsic source ratio may determine image-zone height inside defined Layout Grammar rules;
- information modules are not automatically converted into cards;
- selective tinted editorial information areas remain allowed as accents;
- 15 mm technical minimum binding zone;
- Companion restored to and kept at its invariant Editorial-World position;
- Product-DNA documentation and Consistency Gate coverage.

## Explicit non-goals

- no Asset Management;
- no Crop Editor;
- no Focal Point;
- no drag-and-drop layout;
- no free positioning;
- no free image-box sizing;
- no gallery;
- no EXIF workflow;
- no new layout variant;
- no new Editorial World;
- no user-selectable page backgrounds or themes.

## Product-DNA check

- Travelbook remains the dominant surface: yes.
- UI speaks Travel Language: yes.
- technical paths remain hidden: yes.
- image geometry help appears only on demand: yes.
- photography is the atmospheric colour source: yes.
- Destination paper remains neutral-white: yes.
- images are composed, not card-mounted: yes.
- content modules are not automatically cardified: yes.
- Publisher remains Publishing truth: yes.
- Companion remains unantastbar: yes.
