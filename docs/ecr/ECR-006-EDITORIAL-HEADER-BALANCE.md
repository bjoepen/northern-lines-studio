# ECR-006 – Editorial Header Balance

## Observation

Real-world use of Build 006 showed that the persistent header content was visually pulled toward the upper edge of the navy bar. The header contained the correct information, but its vertical balance did not match the calm Editorial Desk below it.

## Decision

Build 007 applies **Polish before Power**:

- all three header zones own the full header height and are vertically centered;
- the active Travelbook becomes the primary centered context;
- the Editorial World becomes secondary context;
- the project control becomes compact and quiet;
- no new feature, panel, toolbar or authoring capability is introduced.

## Acceptance

The header must read as part of the Editorial Frame, not as a conventional application toolbar.
