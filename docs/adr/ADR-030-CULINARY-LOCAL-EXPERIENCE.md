# ADR-030 — Culinary & Local Experience

## Decision

Kulinarik & Lokal uses the shared structured `DestinationInterestEntry` foundation. A recommendation is the semantic unit. The user adds and edits recommendations; Studio owns composition.

## Fields

- title
- category
- why
- try
- guidance
- timePrice
- placeReference

## Consequences

The fourth Interest archetype follows the same UX and persistence pattern as Photography, Hiking & Nature and Culture & History. No culinary database or booking logic is introduced.
